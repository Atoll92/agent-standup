# Name and logo

The code of Agent Standup is MIT licensed: you can use it, change it, fork it and sell it. That licence covers the code. It does not give anyone the right to use the project's name or its look as their own. This page says what is fine and what is not.

The name of this project, its logo (the lead with its gold headset) and its characters are how people recognise it. Its author, Double Geste, uses them as the identity of this project and asks that they are not used for anything else.

## Fine, without asking

- Saying that your tool works with Agent Standup, is built on it, or is a plugin, a pack or a translation for it.
- Writing about it, reviewing it, teaching with it, streaming it, and showing screenshots or recordings of the app.
- Using the name and the unmodified logo to point to the project, for example in a list of tools or next to a link.
- Shipping an unmodified build under its own name, for example in a package repository.
- Forking the repository to send a contribution back.

## Not fine without written permission

- Releasing a modified version or a fork as a separate product under the name "Agent Standup", or under a name close enough to be confused with it ("Agent Standup Pro", "AgentStandup AI" and the like).
- Using the logo or the characters as the logo, mascot or branding of another product, company or service.
- Suggesting that your product or service is made, endorsed or sponsored by this project.
- Registering a domain name, a store listing or a social account that uses the name to look like the official one.
- Selling merchandise that carries the logo or the characters.

## If you fork it into your own product

Give it its own name and its own icon, draw or bring your own characters, and say that it is based on Agent Standup. The MIT licence already asks you to keep the copyright notice. The drawing code is MIT like everything else; what this page restricts is using the result to brand something that is not Agent Standup.

## Asking

Not sure whether a use is fine, or want permission for one that is not? Open an issue at https://github.com/Atoll92/agent-standup/issues and say what you have in mind. Sensible requests get a quick yes.
