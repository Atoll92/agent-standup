Drawn by hand

Every character, drawn by hand

Every character was drawn by hand by the illustrator Fabien Coupas, then cut into layers so that it can talk, nod and type in the call. The rooms around them are drawn in the same style: a crypt, a field under the moon, an old house, a graveyard, the roofs of the city, a bell tower, a forest, a witch's kitchen.

What is inside

Eight characters

A pumpkin to lead the call, a vampire for you, and a ghost, a cat, a bat, a wolf, a witch and a skeleton for your agents. Each one has its own room.

360 skeletons

Five hats, four pairs of glasses, two moustaches and three pairs of eyes: when a big team needs more characters, every skeleton is dressed its own way.

They talk and work

Two mouths for talking, hands that type while the agent works, and now and then a laptop with stickers, pulled out for a few seconds.

Updates included

New characters are on the way. Once you have the pack, “Update the pack” in the app fetches them, at no cost.

How to install it

You need the latest version of Agent Standup (VS Code extension, desktop app or command line). Agent Standup does not need to be open when you pay.

Bought from the app

Nothing to do: once you have paid, the pack installs itself and your agents change at once.

Bought on this page, or on another computer

Open Agent Standup, then ⚙ → Characters → Halloween pack → “I already bought it”, and paste your purchase code (it looks like HW-7KQ2-9XPM). It is on the page you see after paying, and on the receipt and the invoice Stripe emails you.

By hand, from the zip

Download the pack below with your code, and unzip it into the folder ~/.agent-standup/packs/halloween (on Windows %USERPROFILE%\.agent-standup\packs\halloween). Then pick it in ⚙ → Characters

Download it again

Your purchase code gives you the pack as many times as you need it.

Questions

Do I need Agent Standup open to buy the pack?

No. Pay on this page whenever you like: your code installs the pack later, on any computer where Agent Standup runs.

I lost my purchase code.

It is on the receipt and the invoice Stripe emailed you after the payment. If you cannot find them, write to us with the email address you paid with. contact.doublegeste@gmail.com

Why can I pay more than 2 €?

2 € is the price of the pack. If you like it, you can choose a higher amount on the payment page: it supports the people who make it. You get exactly the same pack.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If the pack does not work for you, write to us within 14 days of your purchase and we refund you. contact.doublegeste@gmail.com

Can I show the characters in a video or a stream?

Yes, paid or not: screenshots, recordings, streams and talks are fine. What you may not do is share or resell the picture files themselves.

Which apps does it work in?

In Agent Standup on macOS, Windows and Linux: the VS Code extension, the desktop app and the command line, in its latest version.
