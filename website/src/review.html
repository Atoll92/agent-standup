Your agents have prepared your performance review.

Margot, Remy, Iris and Hugo went through your Claude Code history. They counted every “You’re absolutely right”, every 3 AM prompt and every time you hit Esc. They have notes.

Pick your Claude Code sessions folder, or drag it onto this box: ~/.claude/projects
  • Mac: in the folder window, press ⌘ ⇧ G, paste ~/.claude/projects and press Enter. (⌘ ⇧ . shows hidden folders.)
  • Windows: paste %USERPROFILE%\.claude\projects in the address bar of the folder window.
  • Linux: press Ctrl L and type ~/.claude/projects.

🔒 Your sessions never leave your computer. This page reads them in your browser; there is no server to send them to. Your browser may say “upload”: nothing is uploaded, and you can check the Network tab. Big histories take a minute. On a phone? Look at the sample, then open this page on the computer you code on.
