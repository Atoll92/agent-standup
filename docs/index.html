Your AI agents,

See who is waiting on whom and who is blocked, at zero token cost.

Agent Standup turns your Claude Code, Codex or Mistral Vibe session into a live team meeting: the lead gives orders, sub-agents join, share their screen and report back. It’s a bit silly. It’s also weirdly useful.

(An illustration. The real thing is live, has voices, and follows your actual session.)

It works wherever you run Claude Code, Codex or Mistral Vibe.

What you actually get

  • A live call.
  • One status per agent.
  • Sub-agents join, share their screen, leave.
  • Context gauge and tokens.
  • Replay, recap, tree, video.
  • Voices.
  • Your own characters.
  • 8 languages.
